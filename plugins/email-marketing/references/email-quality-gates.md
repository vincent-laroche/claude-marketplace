# Email quality gates

## Content

- Use approved source copy or clearly mark draft copy.
- Follow Hair Solutions Co. voice: plain-spoken, calm, confident, educational; relief, not rescue.
- Say system, never wig or toupee. Avoid pity, shame, hype, scarcity, emoji, and exclamation marks.
- Verify every product, price, discount, shipping, return, support, and timing claim against authority.
- Resolve every personalization token for known, blank, malformed, and missing values.

## Structure and rendering

- Use email-safe table structure and inline critical CSS.
- Validate 600px desktop and 320px mobile behavior, image blocking, dark mode, and reduced motion.
- Include descriptive alt text, explicit dimensions, meaningful links, and a plain-text equivalent.
- Keep HTML below Gmail clipping risk and make the first meaningful content useful without images.
- Use MailerLite merge syntax only; remove HubSpot or other platform leftovers.

## Compliance and tracking

- Include the MailerLite unsubscribe token and required sender identity/address fields.
- Confirm the campaign subscription purpose matches the selected group or automation trigger.
- Verify UTM parameters, link destinations, preference-center behavior, and suppression rules.
- Treat opens as directional because privacy features distort them; prioritize clicks, conversions,
  complaints, unsubscribes, bounces, and revenue where attribution is valid.

## Release evidence

- Re-fetch campaign or automation state after upload.
- Confirm sender, reply-to, subject, preheader, content type, audience, and tracking settings.
- Capture a mobile and desktop preview or test-render artifact.
- Record untested clients and unresolved dynamic ecommerce blocks.
- Do not call the work released until the explicitly approved send, schedule, or activation is verified.
