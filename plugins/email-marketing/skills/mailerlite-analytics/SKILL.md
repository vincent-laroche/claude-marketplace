# MailerLite analytics

1. Run email-marketing-preflight and verify time zone, date range, population, campaign type, and
   account before comparing metrics.
2. Prefer sent-campaign and automation reports, link clicks, ecommerce conversions, complaints,
   unsubscribes, bounces, and subscriber activity. Treat opens as directional.
3. Separate broadcasts from lifecycle automations, delivered from attempted, unique from total, and
   causation from correlation.
4. Compare like cohorts and normalize for audience size, send time, and eligibility. Identify data
   gaps and small samples.
5. Return: executive finding, metric table, anomalies, likely explanations, confidence, recommended
   action, test design, and safety constraints.

Do not create segments, resends, campaigns, or experiments from an analytics request alone.
