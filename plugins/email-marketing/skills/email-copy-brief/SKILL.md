# Email copy brief

1. Run email-marketing-preflight for any brief tied to a live campaign or automation.
2. Read ../../references/authority-map.md and ../../references/email-quality-gates.md.
3. Identify objective, lifecycle moment, audience, subscription purpose, trigger, exit, and one primary
   action. Separate operational email from marketing email.
4. Use approved copy in emails_master when it exists. Preserve exact approved claims and record its
   source. Mark new work as draft.
5. Produce: internal name, audience, objective, send context, subject options, preview text, message
   hierarchy, body copy, CTA label and destination, personalization fallbacks, assets, proof needed,
   suppression, and open questions.
6. Check voice and claim safety against the live brand repository. Never invent an offer or policy.

Do not create a campaign or send anything from this skill unless the user separately asks for that
named action.
