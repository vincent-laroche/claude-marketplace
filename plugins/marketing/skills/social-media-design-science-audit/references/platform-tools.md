# Platform Tools And Current Checks

Use this reference to select platform-aware tools and avoid overclaiming.

## Primary Platforms

### Instagram

Audit for:

- square, portrait, Story/Reel, and carousel safe zones;
- first slide standalone value;
- Reels hook and captions;
- profile-grid crop risk;
- alt text where available;
- CTA in caption or native sticker, not multiple competing CTAs;
- saves/shares for education and proof, profile visits/clicks for commercial intent.

### TikTok

Audit for:

- 9:16 video, bottom UI zone, and right action-column clearance;
- visual hook in the first 1-2 seconds;
- captions for speech;
- direct, human overlay language;
- no platform-trend text that changes the claim;
- retention, completion, rewatch, shares, comments, and click behavior.

### Facebook

Audit for:

- image/video clarity in feed and ad placements;
- accessible copy and contrast;
- link preview quality;
- paid creative CTA clarity;
- older-audience readability and no tiny overlay text;
- comments/DM quality and click-through by audience.

### YouTube

Audit for:

- thumbnail legibility at 320px wide;
- max 3 major visual elements;
- accurate title/thumbnail promise;
- specific search-intent title;
- chapters for longer videos;
- CTR, retention, and next-video behavior.

## Tool Stack

- Meta Ads Guide: placement specs and format constraints.
- Meta Creative Hub / Ads Manager preview: mockups, placement previews, paid creative QA.
- TikTok Creative Center: top ads, trend discovery, creative insights, and platform-native references.
- TikTok Ads Manager / Creative tools: paid creative previews and diagnostics.
- Canva/Figma/Adobe Express: production, but not proof of platform fitness.
- CapCut: short-form editing, captions, and platform-native video iteration.
- Attention Insight / similar attention-prediction tools: directional pre-launch attention estimates only.
- Native analytics: the strongest proof for performance after publishing.

## Evidence Strength

1. Real platform analytics and experiments.
2. Platform preview/ad preview and native placement checks.
3. Exported asset dimension/safe-zone checks.
4. Accessibility checks: captions, alt text, legibility, flashing risk.
5. Expert heuristic/design review.
6. AI attention predictions or inspiration-library comparisons.
