# Social Design Science

Use this reference for social creative audits across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, and paid social.

## Attention And First-Frame Science

Social creative competes in a fast, thumb-driven environment. Audit:

- the first frame communicates a concrete reason to stop;
- the subject is visible before the viewer reads captions;
- one dominant idea appears per frame/slide;
- visual hierarchy makes the next action obvious;
- unnecessary labels, badges, and decorative marks do not compete with the face/product/proof;
- hooks are useful, not misleading.

For video, the opening 1-2 seconds should show a useful visual: product detail, hairline, base material, founder, client-approved proof, or a buyer question.

## UI/UX And Composition

Use transferable UI/UX principles:

- clear figure/ground separation;
- enough contrast after compression;
- proximity and grouping;
- consistent reading path;
- safe-zone margins;
- no dense tables or website-length copy;
- no equal-weight competing CTAs;
- captions and graphic text support each other instead of duplicating clutter.

## Color Science

Use brand colors as roles, not decoration:

- measure contrast for overlay text and CTA chips;
- protect product/hair color accuracy;
- avoid large decorative accents that distort perceived realism;
- reserve high-chroma or metallic accent colors for small cues;
- check social exports in sRGB and after compression.

Invoke `color-science-palette-audit` for deeper palette compatibility, contrast, and brand-drift checks.

## Accessibility

Minimum social accessibility review:

- alt text where supported;
- captions for spoken video;
- sound-off comprehension;
- text large enough for feed preview and mobile viewing;
- no fast flashing/strobing;
- no important information only in fast-moving text;
- clear link/CTA purpose;
- color is not the only way status or emphasis is communicated.

## Claim And Proof Safety

High-risk claims include guaranteed, invisible, permanent, cure, restore growth, zero maintenance, works for everyone, nobody will know, miracle, and shame-based framing.

For testimonials and before/after assets:

- require written consent for quote/image/channel;
- keep attribution within approved limits;
- do not alter meaning;
- keep lighting/crop fair;
- include context where results depend on fit, base type, density, maintenance, and application.

## Platform-Native Fit

- Instagram: premium trust, visual consistency, carousels, Reels, Stories, profile-grid coherence.
- TikTok: conversational, direct, useful, less polished, demonstration-led, human language.
- Facebook: clear proof, useful captions, accessible copy, paid/ad clarity, older-device readability.
- YouTube: search intent, thumbnail clarity, accurate title, retention and chapters for longer videos.
