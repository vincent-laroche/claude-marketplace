# Ecommerce UX Audit Criteria

Use this reference for Shopify, DTC, product-page, collection, cart, and checkout-adjacent reviews.

## Page Job

Start every ecommerce audit by naming the user's job:

- learn whether the product solves their problem;
- compare options;
- trust the seller;
- understand fit, compatibility, sizing, lifespan, maintenance, shipping, returns, or support;
- choose a variant or configuration;
- add to cart with confidence;
- recover from uncertainty without leaving.

If a section does not help one of those jobs, question its role.

## Product Detail Pages

Audit:

- product identity above the fold;
- price, availability, and variant clarity;
- realistic media and alt text;
- material, fit, size, maintenance, lifespan, and compatibility details;
- CTA visibility and state feedback;
- trust signals near decisions, not only in footer;
- reviews, FAQs, returns, shipping, and support context;
- sticky/add-to-cart behavior on mobile;
- option selection friction and error prevention;
- structured data and crawlable product content.

## Collection And Listing Pages

Audit:

- category purpose and H1 clarity;
- filtering and sorting relevance;
- product-card comparison attributes;
- image consistency and useful crop;
- price and variant signals;
- empty state and no-results recovery;
- mobile filter access without obstructive overlays;
- pagination/infinite-scroll behavior;
- internal links to education and buying guidance.

## Navigation And Information Architecture

Audit:

- top-level categories match customer language;
- navigation supports both browsing and known-item search;
- breadcrumbs and context are visible where helpful;
- help/education content is findable from decision points;
- footer is useful without carrying the whole IA burden;
- search has useful empty, loading, and result states.

## Cart And Checkout-Adjacent UX

Audit:

- cart summary clarity;
- editable quantity/variant state;
- shipping, returns, support, and discount expectations;
- error prevention and recovery;
- trust and payment reassurance;
- no surprise blockers before checkout;
- accessibility and keyboard access in drawers/modals.

## Mobile Ecommerce

Audit at 320, 375, 390, and 430px when possible:

- primary controls are reachable and about 44px or larger;
- sticky elements do not obscure content or each other;
- variant controls and filters do not create layout shifts;
- media, CTA, price, and trust content remain discoverable;
- text does not overflow buttons/cards;
- drawers and modals trap focus correctly and close predictably.

## Hair Solutions Co. Specific Notes

For hair replacement systems:

- trust depends on realism, fit, privacy, support, and education;
- avoid before/after framing, pity, hype, or urgency pressure;
- prioritize practical answers: fit, base, hair type, density, lifespan, attachment, maintenance, shipping, returns, and appointments;
- use calm, plain language and realistic media;
- treat product options and custom configurators as high-risk UX surfaces.
