# Higgsfield Soul ID

Read `../../references/production-contract.md`. Use Soul ID for a specific real person; use `../higgsfield-soul-cast` for a fictional actor designed from parameters.

## Prepare the training set

- Collect at least 20 recent, high-quality photos of the same consenting person.
- Favor even, clear lighting. Exclude sunglasses, heavy shadows, cropped faces, beauty filters, and severe compression.
- Include varied angles and natural expressions so the model learns a face rather than one pose.
- Include at least one full-height image to improve body proportions.
- Remove duplicates and images where hairstyle, facial hair, age, or appearance conflicts with the intended identity master.
- Preserve the original set and record the person's consent and intended uses.

Quality and internal consistency matter more than adding marginal images.

## Train and establish the master

1. Train one identity and give it a stable production name.
2. Generate neutral validation images in Soul 2.0 before testing dramatic styles.
3. Check jawline, eyes, nose, skin tone, hairline, facial hair, age, and body proportion.
4. Reject the identity if multiple neutral outputs look merely similar rather than clearly the same person.
5. Approve a small set of master outputs: neutral portrait, three-quarter portrait, full-height frame, and the primary delivery crop.

## Direct with presets and controlled variation

Use Soul 2.0 presets to establish the visual world while Soul ID protects identity. Change one production dimension at a time: wardrobe, location, lighting, camera, or aesthetic. Do not repeatedly redescribe facial features that the trained identity should own.

For a professional founder asset, begin with restrained wardrobe, direct eye contact, natural skin texture, credible office or studio context, and uncluttered framing. Build alternate outfits and locations only after the neutral founder master passes.

## Know the limits

Soul ID provides strong identity continuity, not pixel-identical faces. Extreme angles, aggressive stylization, old or inconsistent training photos, and occlusion can still introduce drift. The identity remains a Higgsfield asset rather than an exportable standalone model.

Use `../higgsfield-asset-continuity` to promote approved Soul ID outputs into a reusable multi-shot asset system.
