# Higgsfield Marketing Studio

Read `../../references/production-contract.md` first. Marketing Studio is for commercial output; it does not replace product truth, claims review, consent, or publication approval.

## Prepare a source-backed campaign brief

Capture the product/offer, real proof points, audience, platform, aspect ratio, hook, one desired action, approved assets, prohibited claims, and whether the result is a draft or ready for final review. If using a product URL, inspect the live source material and correct any invented details before generating.

## Build a bounded variation set

Choose one format and variable at a time: creator framing, hook, visual proof, objection, or ending. Preserve the product geometry and actual visual identity. Keep unverified claims, before/after promises, artificial urgency, and medical or guaranteed-result language out of prompts and captions.

## Review before distribution

Review the first completed variation for product accuracy, identity/consent, readable copy, audience fit, and platform-safe crop. Only then expand the set. Creation is not authorization to upload, schedule, post, or embed on hairsolutions.co.

Use `../higgsfield-video` for the actual motion shot and `../higgsfield-canvas` when the campaign needs transparent source routing and reusable branches.
