# Higgsfield Audio

Read `../../references/production-contract.md`. Separate narration, character acting, speech clarity, translation, lip-sync, music, effects, and ambience before choosing a model.

## Route the task

- **Eleven v3:** audition first for expressive voice generation and characterful narration.
- **MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD:** audition for clean high-definition speech synthesis.
- **VibeVoice:** audition for voice-generation alternatives when vocal character is the key variable.
- **Seed Speech:** audition for speech-synthesis alternatives and language fit.
- **Seed Audio 1.0:** use for sound design, ambience, and scenes that need several speakers or environmental sound.

These are selection starting points, not promises about every voice or language. Use the same short script to audition plausible speech models and compare pronunciation, emotion, pacing, noise, and editability.

## Prepare the source

- Use a clean final script with pronunciation notes, pauses, names, and number formatting.
- Use high-quality, noise-free WAV or MP3 for custom voice or voice change.
- Obtain explicit consent before cloning or transforming an identifiable voice.
- Match delivery to the character, audience, and scene rather than choosing a voice in isolation.
- For translation and lip-sync, use footage where the target face remains clear and unobstructed.

## Review

Listen for pronunciation, breath artifacts, prosody, emphasis, room tone, speaker consistency, translation meaning, lip closure, and sync at cuts. Review the audio with the final picture and music; a convincing voice alone can still be wrong for the scene.
