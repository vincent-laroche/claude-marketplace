# Magnific audio and voice production

## Choose the audio job

| Need | Route |
|---|---|
| Narration or spoken script | TTS / Voice Generator |
| Voice choice and attributes | Current voice catalog and voice details |
| Original music bed | Music generation |
| Single sound or ambience | Sound-effect generation |
| Pull a specified sound out of media | Audio Isolation |
| Make a video speak | Video-speech generation, coordinated with video production |

## Voice safeguards

- Use only a voice the user owns, is authorised to use, or that the platform supplies for that use.
- Do not impersonate a real identifiable person, infer consent, or present synthetic audio as their unqualified statement.
- Treat medical, pricing, performance, and product claims as copy requiring source review before narration.
- Keep final script, chosen voice, language, pronunciation guidance, tone, duration target, and intended destination together in the work order.

## Safe sequence

1. Read `magnific-production-safety`; inspect current voice/model availability and constraints with the supported tools.
2. Produce a clean final script before opening generation. Include deliberate pauses and pronunciation notes only when current tool support is verified.
3. Simulate cost and obtain approval for the exact voice, take count, duration, and destination.
4. Render a short proof when voice fit, pronunciation, or pacing is not already approved.
5. Review intelligibility, pronunciation, pacing, emotional tone, clipping, duration, and claim accuracy.
6. File accepted audio with source script and version. For video, confirm sync and ensure the intended audio is the only audio owner for that beat.

Never assume generated music or stock-like sounds are cleared for a particular commercial use. Verify current platform licensing before delivery or publication.

Use `magnific-video-production` for video timing/combining, and `magnific-spaces-and-flows` when audio is wired into a repeatable graph.
