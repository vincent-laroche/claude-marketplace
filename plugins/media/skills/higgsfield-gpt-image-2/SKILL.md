# Higgsfield GPT Image 2

Read `../../references/production-contract.md`. Prefer GPT Image 2 when photorealism, commercial product presentation, and accurate visible text are the leading requirements.

## Direct the image

Write the brief in this order:

- product or subject truth;
- setting and composition;
- material behavior and contact shadows;
- light source, contrast, and color temperature;
- camera and depth of field;
- exact visible copy and language;
- protected references and exclusions.

Use one clean reference image to guide the subject, character, or environment when needed. Do not ask a style reference to silently redefine product geometry.

## Choose between neighboring models

- Use GPT Image 2 for photoreal product imagery, signage, posters, packaging, and multilingual text.
- Use Nano Banana Pro for reasoning-heavy scene structure and complex object relationships.
- Use Seedream Pro for surgical edits, dense infographics, group-photo integration, and broad multilingual typography.

## Review

Inspect material realism, edge quality, reflections, logos, exact spelling, kerning, language-specific glyphs, product proportions, and whether the result still looks credible at delivery size. Keep regulated or frequently revised copy native.
