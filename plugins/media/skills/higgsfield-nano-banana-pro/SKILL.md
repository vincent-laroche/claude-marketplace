# Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro

Read `../../references/production-contract.md`. Use Nano Banana Pro when structure and instruction fidelity matter as much as appearance.

## Build visual logic first

Specify:

1. subject and intended use;
2. exact objects, count, roles, and positions;
3. foreground, middle ground, and background;
4. lighting, focus, and camera;
5. exact visible text and placement;
6. identity or product references;
7. elements that must not change.

Use concise numbered constraints for complex scenes. Treat the model as a layout-and-logic stage: establish who stands where, which objects matter, what the hierarchy is, and where copy appears before sending the accepted frame into storyboarding, editing, or video.

## Choose Pro versus Nano Banana 2

- Choose Pro for maximum prompt obedience, reasoning-guided composition, exact object relationships, and high-value master frames.
- Choose Nano Banana 2 for faster production-scale iteration, localization, and variations when its output already meets the accuracy bar.

## Review

Count every required object, read every text string, compare identity and product geometry, verify left/right and foreground/background instructions, and inspect hands and contact points. Keep final legal, medical, transactional, and interface copy native when editability or absolute accuracy is required.
