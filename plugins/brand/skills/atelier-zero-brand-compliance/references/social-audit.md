# Social media series audit

Read the canonical voice rules and `specs/PLATFORM_SOCIAL.md`. Audit both each asset and the sequence.

## Evidence

Inspect:

- every final frame or export;
- captions, hooks, hashtags, alt text, and CTA copy;
- platform/canvas dimensions and safe zones;
- the feed crop or thumbnail at 320px;
- the complete carousel, Story set, Reel cover, or campaign order;
- the recent nine-tile grid when the request includes feed consistency;
- consent and live product-fact evidence where applicable.

If only a concept or copy deck exists, audit that scope and mark export, compression, safe-zone, and media checks `NOT VERIFIED`.

## Core composition

- One idea per asset.
- Choose product, person, or message; do not force all three into one frame.
- Whitespace remains active; static frames use at most one headline and one short supporting line.
- Paper-family surface, Ink typography, and at most one Coral signal dominate.
- Coral remains under roughly 10% and is not small body text.
- Ink is the rare full-dark interruption.
- The logo is accurate, secondary, and inside the safe zone.

## Platform geometry

- Instagram square: 1080×1080 with 160px inset.
- Instagram portrait: 1080×1350 with 160px top/bottom and 80px side safe zones.
- Story/Reel/Short: 1080×1920 with the platform UI zones kept clear.
- TikTok video: keep the bottom 220px clear.
- YouTube thumbnail: 1280×720 and readable at 320px.
- YouTube banner: key content inside the centered 1546×423 safe area.
- Carousel slide one works independently.
- Spoken video includes captions; fast flashing and unreadable fast-moving text fail.

## Series and grid

- No adjacent Ink tiles in the rolling nine-tile grid unless an explicitly documented launch arc justifies the temporary sequence.
- Product and human/lifestyle moments remain represented across the rolling grid.
- No repeated Coral trim or templated sameness across every card/frame.
- Carousel logic is coherent and every slide advances one idea.
- Reels have separate feed-readable covers when required.

## Type and copy

- Use approved brand families; only documented Canva fallbacks are acceptable.
- No novelty, script, condensed hype, or distressed type.
- Hooks are useful and specific, not manipulative or clickbait.
- No emoji, exclamation marks, shame, pity, fake urgency, medical framing, cure/permanent/invisible/zero-maintenance/universal-fit claims, or guaranteed transformation.
- Captions add context rather than repeating the graphic.
- Hashtags are restrained and relevant; never use a generic 30-tag block.

## Truth, consent, and export

- Product names, specs, prices, and availability are verified immediately before scheduling.
- Testimonials, DMs, customer questions, images, before/after media, and voice clips are real and approved for the exact channel/use.
- Lighting, skin, hairline, density, base texture, and result remain truthful.
- Export is sRGB and survives mobile preview/platform compression.
- Alt text exists where supported.
- Do not claim performance without analytics or a controlled comparison.

Any missing consent or fabricated testimonial/result is a blocker. Any stale live product fact remains `NOT VERIFIED` until refreshed.
